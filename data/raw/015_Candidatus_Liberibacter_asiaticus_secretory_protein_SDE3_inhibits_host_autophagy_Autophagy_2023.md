'Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus' secretory protein SDE3 inhibits host autophagy to promote Huanglongbing disease in citrus
来源：Autophagy 2023, 19(9), 2558-2574（Taylor & Francis），作者：Jinxia Shi、Yinan Gong、Hongwei Shi、Xiaoding Ma、Yuanhong Zhu、Fan Yang、Dan Wang、Yating Fu、Yu Lin、Naiying Yang、Zhuhui Yang、Chunhua Zeng、Weimin Li、Changyong Zhou、Xuefeng Wang、Yongli Qiao（上海师范大学生命科学学院上海市植物分子科学重点实验室；西南大学柑桔研究所国家柑桔工程技术研究中心；中国农业科学院作物科学研究所；中国农业科学院植物保护研究所；北京农学院），DOI：10.1080/15548627.2023.2213040

摘要：Antimicrobial acroautophagy/autophagy plays a vital role in degrading intracellular pathogens or microbial molecules in host-microbe interactions. However, microbes evolved various mechanisms to hijack or modulate autophagy to escape elimination. Vector-transmitted phloem-limited bacteria, Candidatus Liberibacter (Ca. Liberibacter) species, cause Huanglongbing (HLB), one of the most catastrophic citrus diseases worldwide, yet contributions of autophagy to HLB disease proliferation remain poorly defined. Here, we report the identification of a virulence effector in "Ca. Liberibacter asiaticus" (Las), SDE3, which is highly conserved among the "Ca. Liberibacter". SDE3 expression not only promotes the disease development of HLB and canker in sweet orange (Citrus sinensis) plants but also facilitates Phytophthora and viral infections in Arabidopsis, and Nicotiana benthamiana (N. benthamiana). SDE3 directly associates with citrus cytosolic glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenases (CsGAPCs), which negatively regulates plant immunity. Overexpression of CsGAPCs and SDE3 significantly inhibits autophagy in citrus, Arabidopsis, and N. benthamiana. Intriguingly, SDE3 undermines autophagy-mediated immunity by the specific degradation of CsATG8 family proteins in a CsGAPC1-dependent manner. CsATG8 degradation is largely rescued by treatment with an inhibitor of the late autophagic pathway, E64d. Furthermore, ectopic expression of CsATG8 enhances Phytophthora resistance. Collectively, these results suggest that SDE3-CsGAPC interactions modulate CsATG8-mediated autophagy to enhance Las progression in citrus.

中文概括：本研究鉴定了柑橘黄龙病菌（Las）分泌的效应蛋白SDE3，揭示其致病机制。SDE3在韧皮部杆菌属中高度保守，表达后不仅促进甜橙黄龙病和溃疡病发展，还增强拟南芥和本氏烟对疫霉及病毒的感病性。SDE3直接结合柑橘胞质甘油醛-3-磷酸脱氢酶CsGAPC1/CsGAPC2（植物免疫负调控因子），二者共同显著抑制柑橘、拟南芥和本氏烟的细胞自噬。SDE3以CsGAPC1依赖方式特异性降解CsATG8家族蛋白，从而破坏自噬介导的免疫；该降解可被自噬晚期通路抑制剂E64d显著挽救。过表达CsATG8则增强对疫霉的抗性。研究首次阐明SDE3-CsGAPC-CsATG8模块调控自噬以促进Las侵染的分子机制。
